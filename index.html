How do I get a key? (Microsoft Azure Speech has a free tier)
  1. Sign in at portal.azure.com (you need a free Azure account).
  2. Click “Create a resource”, search for Speech, and create it. Choose the Free (F0) pricing tier if offered.
  3. Open the new resource and go to Keys and Endpoint.
  4. Copy KEY 1 and the Location/Region (for example eastus) and paste them above.

Free-tier limits and sign-up requirements are set by Microsoft and can change. Your text is sent directly from your browser to Microsoft's speech service to create the audio. It does not pass through any other server.

When you make a mistake: stop for about a second, press M, then re-read the whole sentence. Use headphones and record in a quiet, soft room (curtains, sofa, close to the mic). That makes the biggest difference to the final sound.
